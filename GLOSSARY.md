# Bliss Pulse Clinic Booking

Terms for private client registration and appointment booking at Bliss Pulse Neurotherapy Clinic.

## Language

**Client**:
A person with an individual Bliss code and private clinical record.
_Avoid_: Patient record, group client

**Attendee**:
A **Client** included in a **Group Booking**.
_Avoid_: Group member, participant

**Booking Contact**:
The person named to coordinate a **Group Booking** and receive its communications. A **Booking Contact** may also be an **Attendee**.
_Avoid_: Group leader, organiser

**Group Booking**:
A set of individual **Attendees** linked to one **Booking Contact** for coordinated scheduling.
_Avoid_: Group client, shared client

**Individual Appointment**:
One scheduled treatment session for one **Client**.
_Avoid_: Group appointment, shared session
